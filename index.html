Slipchute is a calm sorting puzzle for iPhone. One swipe tilts the whole tray, every parcel slides until something stops it, and each one has to drop into the chute with its own stamp.

How it plays

One swipe. Everything slides.

  1. 1

    Tilt the tray

    Swipe, or tap the arrows. Every parcel on the tray slides that way at once.

  2. 2

    Use the stoppers

    Walls, paperweights and other parcels stop a slide. Line each parcel up with its chute.

  3. 3

    Watch the stamps

    Each tilt cancels a postage stamp. Deliver every parcel before the strip runs out.

What it is not

No clock. No queue. No ads.

No timer

Think as long as you like. The limit is tilts, not seconds.

No lives

Retry any route straight away, as often as you want.

No ads

Nothing interrupts a route, and nothing tracks you.

Offline

Plays anywhere. Your progress stays on your iPhone.

FAQ

Questions at the counter

Is Slipchute free?

The first chapter, Front Counter, is free: 30 full routes. All Routes is a one-time purchase that opens the other 60.

What do the tool packs do?

Desk Drawer and Supply Cabinet add Undo, +3 Tilts and Lift to your stock. They are optional; every route can be solved without them.

I bought All Routes on another device.

Open Settings → Restore Purchases with the same Apple Account and it comes back.

Does it collect any data?

No. No account, no ads, no analytics. Progress and purchases are stored on your iPhone only. See the privacy policy.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Only buying or restoring talks to the App Store.

Can I make a route easier?

Yes: Undo takes back a tilt, +3 Tilts extends the strip, and Lift picks a parcel off the tray (that route then earns one star).
