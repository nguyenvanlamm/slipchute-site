How to play

Swipe, or tap the arrows, to tilt the whole tray. Every parcel slides that way until a wall, a paperweight or another parcel stops it. A parcel that lines up with a chute of its own stamp drops in. Deliver every parcel before the strip of postage stamps (your tilts) runs out. Undo takes back a tilt, +3 Tilts adds three, and Lift picks a parcel off the tray (that route then earns one star).

Purchases

Front Counter (30 routes) is free. All Routes opens Back Room and Night Shift once, for good; Desk Drawer and Supply Cabinet are optional tool packs. Bought something before, or on another device with the same store account? Open Settings → Restore Purchases.

Progress

Progress is saved on the device only. It is not synced between devices, and it is removed if you delete the app.
