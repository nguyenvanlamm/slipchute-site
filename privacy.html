Privacy Policy

Slipchute · last updated 26 October 2026

Slipchute is a puzzle game. It does not ask who you are, and it does not sell any data about you.

What stays on your device

  • Your progress: the stars you earned on each route.
  • Your tools: how many Undo, +3 Tilts and Lift you have left.
  • Whether you have bought All Routes, and the store transaction ids already delivered (so a purchase is never counted twice).

This is stored on your device only. Deleting the app removes it.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking of any kind.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

Purchases

The first chapter, Front Counter, is free. Purchases (All Routes, Desk Drawer, Supply Cabinet) are handled entirely by the App Store under its own privacy policy. We receive no payment details; the game only learns from the store which purchases exist.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
